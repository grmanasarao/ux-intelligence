Synthetic UX research interview created for Phase 3 dataset development.

Moderator: Today we're looking at the checkout experience. Please purchase a pair of running shoes, but stop before payment.
Participant: Okay. I usually compare a few shoes before deciding, especially if I don't know the brand.
Moderator: Find a pair suitable for running.
Participant: The search is fast. I can filter by category and size, which is useful.
Moderator: What do you think about the filters?
Participant: They are useful, although there are too many options on the left. I have to scroll quite a lot.
Moderator: Choose a pair and review the product details.
Participant: The photos and specifications are clear. The reviews are helpful because I care about sizing.
Moderator: Could you determine whether your size is available?
Participant: Yes, the size selector shows availability immediately. That's much better than opening another page.
Moderator: Add the shoes to your basket.
Participant: Done. The basket icon changed, but I didn't get a clear message saying the item was added.
Moderator: Is that a problem?
Participant: Not a major one because I can see the basket count, but a short confirmation would make me more confident.
Moderator: Proceed to checkout.
Participant: The checkout is straightforward. Delivery information is near the top and the progress steps make sense.
Moderator: Enter a different delivery address.
Participant: I can edit it, but the address form asks for several fields that don't seem necessary.
Moderator: Which part is confusing?
Participant: There is a field for county and another for region. I don't know which one applies to my address, so I'm not sure what to enter.
Moderator: Continue.
Participant: It accepted the address. The delivery options are clear, but the fastest option is shown first even though it's much more expensive.
Moderator: How would you prefer to see them?
Participant: I'd rather have the cheapest reasonable option highlighted, then show the faster ones.
Moderator: What happens if you go back to the product page?
Participant: I can go back, but I'm worried I'll lose the information I entered. I haven't tried it because I don't want to restart.
Moderator: Overall, how would you rate the checkout?
Participant: Mostly good. The steps are clear and it feels faster than many websites I've used.
Moderator: What should be improved?
Participant: Simplify the address form, make add-to-basket feedback clearer, and explain the delivery price difference more clearly.

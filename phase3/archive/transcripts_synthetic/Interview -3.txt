Synthetic UX research interview created for Phase 3 dataset development.

Moderator: We'll focus on the mobile shopping experience. Please find a skincare product for sensitive skin.
Participant: I normally shop on my phone, so I care a lot about how quickly I can scan the page.
Moderator: Start from the home page.
Participant: The home page has lots of banners. It looks attractive, but I have to scroll quite a bit before I reach useful categories.
Moderator: Find skincare.
Participant: I found it after scrolling. The category menu is not obvious at first.
Moderator: Now find products for sensitive skin.
Participant: There is a skin-type filter, but I had to open the filter panel to discover it.
Moderator: What happened when you applied the filter?
Participant: The results refreshed quickly. That's good.
Moderator: How do you decide which product to open?
Participant: Mostly from the product name and rating. I would also like to see key ingredients on the result card.
Moderator: Open a product.
Participant: The description is long. The important information is mixed with marketing language.
Moderator: What would help?
Participant: A short summary at the top saying who the product is for and what the main ingredients are.
Moderator: Find information about whether it is suitable for sensitive skin.
Participant: I had to read several paragraphs before I found it. It says fragrance-free, which is important to me.
Moderator: Can you find the ingredients list?
Participant: Yes, but it is far down the page. I expected an ingredients tab near the top.
Moderator: Add the product to your basket.
Participant: The button is large and easy to see. I know immediately what to press.
Moderator: Now remove it.
Participant: That's less obvious. I can change the quantity, but I don't immediately see a remove option.
Moderator: What do you do?
Participant: I reduce the quantity to zero, but that just gives me an error. Then I find the remove link.
Moderator: How did that feel?
Participant: A little annoying because I expected the same control to work in both directions.
Moderator: Would you use this website again?
Participant: Yes. The basic shopping actions are easy, and it feels responsive.
Moderator: What would you improve first?
Participant: Make important product information easier to scan, simplify the mobile category navigation, and make removal more obvious.
